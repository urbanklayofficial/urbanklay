04 — The Founder

A chef's precision,
stitched into cloth.

Urban Klay was founded by Ivan Bekcic, a Michelin-trained chef recognized as "Top 30 Under 30" and a "Top 40 Under 40" Forbes nominee.

He moved from the kitchen into fashion and brought the craft with him. Layering a look the way you layer flavour. Technique, intention, nothing wasted.
