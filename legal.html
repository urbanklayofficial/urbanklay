Policies

The fine print, kept simple.

Every Urban Klay piece is made to order for you by our print partners, then shipped to you, wherever you are.

Shipping

Orders are made to order and usually ship within ✏ 2–7 business days. Delivery times after that depend on where you are:

RegionTypical delivery
Canada✏ 3–7 business days
United States✏ 3–7 business days
Rest of world✏ 7–20 business days

You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. Duties and import taxes outside Canada may be charged by your local customs office.

Returns & exchanges

✏ Write your returns policy here. A common one for made-to-order brands: because each piece is printed for you, we can't accept returns for change of mind or the wrong size. If your item arrives damaged or misprinted, email us a photo within 30 days of delivery and we'll replace it at no cost.

Questions? Message us on Instagram or email us any time.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to make and deliver your order and keep you updated: your name, email, shipping address and order details. Payments are processed securely by Stripe; we never see or store your card number. Orders are produced by our print partners (Printful and Tapstitch), who receive only what they need to make and ship your piece.

If you join our list, we'll email you about new drops. Unsubscribe any time with one click. To see or delete the information we hold about you, email us.
